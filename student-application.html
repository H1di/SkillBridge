Student Application

Join as a student and start building practical experience early.

This page turns the report into a simple application structure. The actual live version would collect student details, skills, availability, and portfolio material before team placement and onboarding.

Suggested application fields

  • Full name and contact details
  • University and field of study
  • Current year of study
  • Skills or tools already used
  • Portfolio, GitHub, or project examples
  • Weekly availability and preferred project type

What happens after applying

  • Skill review and matching to project needs
  • Onboarding and expectations briefing
  • Placement into a supervised team
  • Regular project opportunities based on performance and availability

Growth path

Students begin with practical part-time work, build verified outputs, and may later move into stronger responsibility, including mentoring or supervision as the platform grows.

  • Ability to balance project work with study
