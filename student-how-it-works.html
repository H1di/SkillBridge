How It Works

A simple workflow, because business models are already annoying enough without extra chaos.

SkillBridge operates as a service-based platform. Businesses submit project inquiries through the website, administrators review the request, suitable student teams are assigned, supervisors monitor progress, and only reviewed work is delivered to the client.

Platform infrastructure

  • Trello for task assignment and progress tracking
  • Zoom for meetings and team communication
  • IDrive for file delivery and storage
  • GitHub for project records
  • Clockify for student work-hour tracking
  • Bank transfer or Wise for payments

Why this structure works

It keeps the model lean. SkillBridge does not need a huge proprietary platform at the start. It uses existing tools to stay organised, transparent, and scalable while keeping costs lower.

Project flow

1

Business submits inquiry

The client explains the service required through the website contact or intake form.

2

Administrators review scope

The project is assessed, priced, and matched with the most suitable student team.

3

Students deliver under supervision

Teams complete the work remotely while supervisors monitor progress and quality.

4

Client receives reviewed output

Only work that has passed the supervision and quality review process is delivered.

5

Feedback and retention

Clients can request small revisions, provide feedback, and move into repeat or subscription-based work later.
