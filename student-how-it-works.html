Operating Model

A lean service platform with supervision, quality control, and enough structure to avoid predictable mess.

SkillBridge starts with website inquiries, routes work through administrators and supervisors, and assigns student teams based on skill and availability. Everything is organised to balance affordability with reliability.

Project Records

Used for files, version control, and completed work history.

Client journey

1

Inquiry received

Businesses submit project requirements through the website contact form.

2

Consultation and estimate

Administrators consult with client needs, scope the work, and prepare pricing.

3

Team assignment

One or more student teams are formed around project skills and availability.

4

Review and delivery

Supervisors review outputs before final delivery and small revisions can be requested.

Student journey

1

Recruitment

Students are recruited through universities, unions, social media, and career networks.

2

Onboarding

Students are organised into teams and prepared for client-facing project work.

3

Supervised execution

Work is completed remotely with regular updates and mentor oversight.

4

Portfolio growth

Completed work contributes to verified experience and future employability.

Implementation roadmap

Months 1-2

Website development, project infrastructure setup, and cloud hosting preparation.

Months 2-3

Recruit student teams through university partnerships, unions, and social channels.

Months 3-4

Run pilot projects with first startup and SME clients to test the workflow.

Months 4-6

Official platform launch with larger marketing push and broader client acquisition.

Year 2 onward

Scale to more universities, add more teams, and introduce automated pricing and project matching.

Long-term

Develop subscription services, training programmes, and stronger internal infrastructure.
